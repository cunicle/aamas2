\section{Discussion, Limitations, and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\plan{The oracle skeleton fixes the number and names of the calls (free generation: 75.2\% set accuracy for \dream vs 88.0\% with the skeleton at $k{=}2$); two dLLMs; \llada subsets of 100 and 34 requests; greedy decoding only; the one-token slot of the open choose-N variant; no S$^3$-style prompted null baseline; skeleton tokenization differs from free generation. Beyond one agent: parallel decisions are safe when a shared convention assigns roles and collide when roles are symmetric; misleading shared signals mis-bind even sequential decisions; the same holds for multi-agent LLM systems that claim subtasks in parallel.}
